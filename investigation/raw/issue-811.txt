Deliver a bounded trusted node-local L1 cache for ephemeral self-hosted CI jobs, with explicit ownership and no public/fork exposure.

## Required behavior

- Define one supported per-node store/daemon lifecycle across one-job ARC runner pods.
- Preserve Kache's local SQLite/blob correctness under concurrent trusted jobs.
- Isolate trust domains so public, fork, or otherwise untrusted jobs cannot read or mutate the trusted cache.
- Bound disk with GC, reservations, health, and node-local hit/miss/byte metrics.
- Fail open to ordinary per-job L2/v3 behavior when L1 is unavailable.
- Document runner/platform wiring and operational rollback.

## Acceptance

- Warm trusted nodes download only misses.
- Isolation tests prove untrusted jobs receive no node-cache mount/socket/credentials.
- Concurrent-job, daemon restart, corrupt local entry, disk-pressure, and fallback tests.
- Five clean cold-node and warm-node representative runs.
