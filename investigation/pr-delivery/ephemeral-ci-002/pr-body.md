Concurrent CLI pulls in [sync](https://github.com/kunobi-ninja/kache/blob/4ee0f9e2b9673aa16f29c9c7e39bf8d0152671d9/src/cli.rs#L7444-L7495) can replace shared extracted files during another pull’s import, causing missing-file and size-mismatch failures. Hold a cross-process lock for each cache through planning, extraction and import. `concurrent_bulk_sync_clients_hydrate_shared_store_without_errors` covers clean, committed and parallel pulls; `just check` passed.
