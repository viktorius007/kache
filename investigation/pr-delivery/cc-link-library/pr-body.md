[`cc_link_file_inputs`](https://github.com/kunobi-ninja/kache/blob/4ee0f9e2b9673aa16f29c9c7e39bf8d0152671d9/src/compiler/cc.rs#L7727-L7752) ignored `-l` libraries, so opt-in caching could restore a stale executable after its archive changed.
