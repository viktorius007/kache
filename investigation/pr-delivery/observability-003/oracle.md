The regression uses the production writer and reader with an isolated temporary filesystem log. A known first event includes a valid Unicode root. A serialized second event is cut after the first byte of € in its root, which models an actual short-write prefix without a production test hook. One case appends a known next event through log_event; the other leaves the fragment at EOF. The independent oracle is equality with the complete test-owned events that were successfully written, including their order and every field. Whole-log discard, lossy decoding of valid Unicode, loss of either neighbor, or interpreting the broken record as a successful event fails the oracle. Baseline red must be the named invalid-UTF8 recovery expectation, not a compiler or setup failure. A fixture-only discriminator will complete the abandoned record while keeping the expected retained-event list fixed; it must fail because the now-valid abandoned record is required to be retained. The source will then be restored and the final test rerun green. No local cargo-mutants or production hand mutation is performed.
